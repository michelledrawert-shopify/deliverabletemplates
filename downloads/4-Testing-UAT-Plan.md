## 4. Testing / UAT Plan Quality Template

*Partner Deliverable Quality Templates | Focused Plus and mid-market builds | Last updated October 14, 2026*

> Adapt this to your own format. It's a starting point, not a required layout. Match its depth to your project.

### Purpose

Define how the merchant validates that the build supports their real business before launch. The point isn’t that pages load — it’s that the merchant can run the business on Shopify.

| Element | What to define |
| :---- | :---- |
| Objectives | What testing must confirm before launch. |
| Scope and coverage | Storefront, catalog, cart and checkout, customer accounts, order operations, SEO/analytics, apps/integrations, and data-migration validation. Add B2B, POS, or international flows where in scope. Also confirm the Shop sales channel: branding, products, and active discounts appear correctly in the Shop app. |
| Roles | Who owns test scenarios, who tests (merchant business and technical testers), who triages defects, who makes the launch call. |
| Entry criteria | Environment and data ready, integrations testable, access provisioned, scenarios written, partner QA done. |
| Schedule | Test window, tester availability, retest timing, decision date. |
| Defect handling | Severity levels, how defects are logged (with evidence), who fixes, and closure rules. Keep it lightweight, but written down. |
| Exit criteria | What “ready to launch” means: critical paths pass, no open blockers, business sign-off. |

**Quality bar:** coverage spans real customer journeys, operational workflows, integrations, and data — not just screen checks.
