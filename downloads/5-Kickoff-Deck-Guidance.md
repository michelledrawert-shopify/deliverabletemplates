## 5. Kickoff Deck Guidance

*Partner Deliverable Quality Templates | Focused Plus and mid-market builds | Last updated October 14, 2026*

> Adapt this to your own format. It's a starting point, not a required layout. Match its depth to your project.

### Purpose

The kickoff deck aligns everyone at the start. If the SoW, ways of working, and project plan are solid, the deck simply presents them — it isn’t a separate source of truth.

| Element | What to include |
| :---- | :---- |
| Project summary | Build type, plan assumption, goals, success criteria, key dates, major workstreams. |
| Confirmed scope | Scope areas from the SoW, known exclusions, and items still to validate in discovery. |
| Team and roles | Who’s involved on each side and who owns decisions. |
| Ways of working | Cadence, channels, where docs live, response/approval expectations. |
| Milestones | High-level timeline and the immediate next milestones. |
| Risks and open decisions | Known risks, open questions, dependencies, owners, due dates. |
| Next steps | Action items, owners, due dates, and inputs needed from each side. |

**Quality bar:** the deck should create alignment — the right people engaged, scope understood, next decisions visible.
