## 2. Ways of Working and Light Governance Template

*Partner Deliverable Quality Templates | Focused Plus and mid-market builds | Last updated October 14, 2026*

> Adapt this to your own format. It's a starting point, not a required layout. Match its depth to your project.

### Purpose

How the project will be run and how everyone stays aligned — kept proportionate. On a focused build you want enough structure to make decisions and manage risk, not a steering-committee apparatus.

| Element | What to define |
| :---- | :---- |
| Working team and roles | Merchant business owner, merchant technical/decision contact, partner project lead, partner build/solution lead, and any app/integration contact. One person often wears several hats — name who’s accountable, not a formal org chart. |
| Decision ownership and escalation | Who signs off on scope, design, timeline, and launch; plus a single named escalation contact on each side if a decision stalls. |
| Risk and issues | A simple shared list: issue, owner, impact, next step, due date. No formal register required. |
| Change control | How change requests are raised, priced, approved, and reflected in scope/timeline. |
| Launch governance | Go/no-go owner, launch-readiness checklist, who’s on call launch day, and how issues are triaged. |
| Meeting cadence and communication | A regular working check-in, where decisions and notes live (single source of truth), and response-time expectations. |
| If applicable | Light owners for B2B (Plus), POS store operations, or per-market decisions when those are in scope. |

**Quality bar:** a reader can see who owns each decision, how issues get resolved, and how change is controlled — without enterprise overhead.
